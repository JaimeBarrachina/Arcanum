Make one tall portrait card image, aspect ratio about 5:8 (taller than wide), for the skill-tree background of a new psycast path in a RimWorld mod ("Phantom Hand": psychics who summon hands of telekinetic force).

Two references are attached:
1. FIRST: three existing backgrounds from the same psycast system (teal, cyan, indigo). The new card must be drawn EXACTLY the way these are drawn. This is the important reference.
2. SECOND: another add-on path's accepted card, drawn in the same family style in its own colour.

How the family is drawn (follow all of these):
- The image is the card edge to edge, no outer margin.
- A WIDE frame band all around (about 7% of the card width), filled with a muted mid-tone of the theme colour, with thin darker border lines and a row of small dots running along it. A small eye medallion sits centred on the top and bottom edges, a small round knob centred on the left and right edges.
- Inside the frame, a dark panel border, then the main panel FIELD, which is FILLED with a flat, muted, darkish version of the theme colour. The field is not black.
- On the field: faint thin radiating lines, a sparse scatter of tiny star specks and a few small four-pointed sparkles.
- The emblem is made of SOLID BLACK SHAPES laid on the coloured field, big and bold, filling most of the panel and running off its edges, like the swirl, cracks and knots in the first reference. Only a very faint soft rim of light on the black shapes. No neon outlines, no strong glow.
- Flat, graphic, symmetrical. No 3D rendering, no gradients beyond a gentle vignette, no text, no letters.

Theme colour: muted spectral gold / ochre (frame band around #7a6638, field around #3a3020, sparkles a pale warm gold).

Emblem: the open hand. One huge solid black open hand, fingers up and slightly spread, palm toward the viewer, filling the middle of the panel, its wrist dissolving downward into three or four flowing ghostly tails that run off the bottom of the panel. Around it, a thick black ring passing behind the fingers, and four smaller black open hands in the four corners of the panel, tilted outward and partly cut off by the panel edge.
