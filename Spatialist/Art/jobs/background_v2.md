Make one tall portrait card image, aspect ratio about 5:8 (taller than wide), for the skill-tree background of a new psycast path in a RimWorld mod ("Spatialist": psychics who fold space into a personal pocket dimension).

Two references are attached:
1. FIRST: three existing backgrounds from the same psycast system (teal, cyan, indigo). The new card must be drawn EXACTLY the way these are drawn. This is the important reference.
2. SECOND: an earlier draft of our card. Keep only its emblem idea (nested squares receding inward like a tunnel, corners joined by diagonals, two small diamond accents in opposite corners). Do NOT copy its rendering: it was too thin, too black and too glowy.

How the family is drawn (follow all of these):
- The image is the card edge to edge, no outer margin.
- A WIDE frame band all around (about 7% of the card width), filled with a muted mid-tone of the theme colour, with thin darker border lines and a row of small dots running along it. A small eye medallion sits centred on the top and bottom edges, a small round knob centred on the left and right edges.
- Inside the frame, a dark panel border, then the main panel FIELD, which is FILLED with a flat, muted, darkish version of the theme colour (like the teal field behind the Skipmaster swirl). The field is not black.
- On the field: faint thin radiating lines, a sparse scatter of tiny star specks and a few small four-pointed sparkles.
- The emblem is made of SOLID BLACK SHAPES laid on the coloured field, big and bold, filling most of the panel and running off its edges, like the Skipmaster swirl and the Staticlord cracks. Only a very faint soft rim of light on the black shapes. No neon outlines, no strong glow.
- Flat, graphic, symmetrical. No 3D rendering, no gradients beyond a gentle vignette, no text, no letters.

Theme colour: muted amethyst violet (frame band around #5d4a7a, field around #2c2340, sparkles a paler lavender).

Emblem: the folded room. Thick solid black bands forming a large square doorway that nearly fills the panel, with a second and third square nested inside receding toward the centre, the corners joined by thick black diagonal bands so it reads as a tunnel or a cube folded into itself. The coloured field shows through between the bands. Two smaller black diamond-shaped folded squares in the top-left and bottom-right corners, partly cut off by the panel edge.
